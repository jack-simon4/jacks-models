How the MLB Model Works

The model evaluates each game from both sides of the battery. On the pitching side it uses FIP (Fielding Independent Pitching) and WHIP to measure how well a starter limits baserunners independent of defense. Lower FIP means fewer expected runs allowed.

On the offensive side, the model uses wOBA (Weighted On-Base Average) and ISO (Isolated Power) for each lineup to measure true offensive output. These are then adjusted for the ballpark being played in — a game at Coors Field is modeled differently than one at Petco Park.

The final run totals are weighted against the opposing pitcher and defense, giving you a projected score with win probability for each team.
