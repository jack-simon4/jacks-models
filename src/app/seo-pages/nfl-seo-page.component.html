How the NFL Model Works

The model processes 14 team-level stats for all 32 NFL teams, updated weekly after each game. Rushing yards per attempt and passing yards per attempt are compared against opponent defensive averages to project how many yards each offense will generate.

Yards are then converted to points using each team's offensive yards per point — a measure of scoring efficiency. Teams that produce the same yards but score more (fewer penalties, better red zone conversion) are reflected as more efficient.

At the start of a new season, the model blends current-season data with last year's stats to avoid over-weighting small early-season samples. As more games are played, the blend shifts entirely to current data.

When Is NFL Season?

The NFL regular season runs September through January, with the playoffs extending through the Super Bowl in February. The model is most accurate mid-season (Weeks 5–18) once teams have established reliable statistical profiles. All 32 teams are supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are NFL team stats updated?

Stats are automatically updated every Monday and Tuesday to capture Sunday games and Monday Night Football.

Does the model include injuries?

Injuries are not currently modeled explicitly. The stats reflect each team's recent performance, which inherently captures the effect of key player absences over the last several games.

Can I use this for playoff games?

Yes — select any two NFL teams in the scoreboard tool regardless of the type of game.
