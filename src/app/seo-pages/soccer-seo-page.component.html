How the Soccer Model Works

Soccer prediction is uniquely difficult because low-scoring games mean a single defensive error can invalidate an otherwise accurate model. Jack's Models tackles this with expected goals (xG) — a shot-quality metric that measures how many goals a team should have scored based on shot location, type, and defensive pressure, rather than just how many they actually scored.

The model combines xG with goals scored, goals conceded, and possession percentage to build an offensive and defensive strength rating for each club. These ratings are then matched against each other to project a final expected scoreline.

Home field advantage is built in — home sides in the EPL score meaningfully more goals per game than away sides, and the model applies a per-team home adjustment based on historical data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Tie No Bet" mean?

Tie No Bet is a common soccer betting market where your stake is refunded if the match ends in a draw. The model hits 68% on this market, meaning it correctly identifies the winning side in non-draw matches at a solid rate.

Does the model predict over/under goals?

The model outputs a projected scoreline for both teams, which gives you an implied total. You can compare this directly to a bookmaker's Over/Under line.

Can I predict Champions League games?

Yes — select any two clubs from the soccer scoreboard to run a prediction. The tool works for any club matchup regardless of competition.
