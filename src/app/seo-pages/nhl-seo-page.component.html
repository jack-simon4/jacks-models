How the NHL Model Works

Hockey scoring is low-variance, which makes goalie quality one of the most important predictive inputs. The model incorporates save percentage and goals-against average for starting goalies to estimate how many shots translate to goals.

On the offensive side, goals scored per game, shots on goal, and power play percentage are combined to produce an offensive output rating for each team. The model also factors in Corsi (shot attempt differential) as a proxy for puck possession and territorial control.

Home ice advantage in hockey is among the strongest in professional sports, and the model applies a per-team home adjustment based on historical scoring differentials.

When Is NHL Season?

The NHL regular season runs October through April, with the Stanley Cup Playoffs extending through June. The model tracks all 32 teams across both the regular season and playoffs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the model know which goalie is starting?

You can select a starting goalie for each team from the scoreboard tool. The goalie's save percentage and GAA are factored into the projected goal totals.

Why does NHL have the largest sample size?

The NHL model has been tracking predictions the longest — 671 games across multiple seasons — giving it the most statistically reliable accuracy figures of any sport on the site.

Can the model predict overtime results?

The model projects regulation scoring. Overtime and shootout outcomes are essentially coin-flips and are not separately modeled.
