About Jack's Models

Jack's Models is a free statistical sports prediction website. The site uses data-driven computer models to project scores, win probabilities, and spread edges for NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football (NCAAF), college basketball (NCAAM), Soccer, and College Baseball games.

The site was founded by Jack Simon — a Virginia Tech student who built the first model in 2020 using college basketball efficiency ratings. After three years of building, testing, and refining the models, Jack's Models launched publicly on January 17, 2024. All projections are generated by statistical models, not human analysts.

Every model is updated daily using current-season data. Projections are available for any matchup via the Scoreboard, and graded results are tracked on the Results page. Current projections are published on the Weekly Picks page.

Sports Covered

  • NFL — rushing efficiency, passing efficiency, home field advantage
  • NBA — offensive/defensive ratings, pace, recent form
  • MLB — ERA, WHIP, run scoring, bullpen and lineup data
  • NHL — goals for/against, shot metrics, goalie save percentage
  • NCAAF — SOS-weighted yards per play, 136 FBS teams
  • NCAAM — adjusted offensive/defensive efficiency (KenPom-style)
  • Soccer / EPL — expected goals (xG), possession, goals per game
  • College Baseball — ERA, run scoring, ballpark factors

Injuries & Limitations

If a player has missed several games, that will generally be reflected in the statistical data. However, a star player missing one game will not automatically change the model's projection. Be mindful of recent injuries, trades, and lineup changes that the model may not yet reflect.
