About Jack's Models

Started by Jack Simon in his dorm room at Virginia Tech, Jack's Models were built with the purpose of accurately predicting the outcomes of various sporting events. The very first model was created in 2020 using college basketball efficiency ratings. After 3 years of building, testing, and modifying the models, Jack's Models were published online on January 17, 2024. Both the models and the site will continue to be tested and improved throughout the years. Please feel free to reach out to me with any questions, concerns, or advice. I hope you enjoy.

Injuries

If a player has missed a handful of games, that will be reflected in the data since a lot of the data used is from the last 3 games. However, a star player sitting out one game will not effect the data and therefore the model will not change. Be mindful of recent injuries, trades, and healthy scratches.
