How the Model Works

The World Cup model is a neutral-site adaptation of Jack's soccer prediction engine. It blends each team's pre-tournament historical stats with live group stage results using a weighted average — so predictions get sharper as the tournament progresses. More group games played = more weight on current-tournament data.

FAQ

When are predictions updated?

Every morning at 8 AM ET, the model pulls overnight results and recalculates each team's rating before the day's matches.

Does the model handle penalty shootouts?

The model predicts 90-minute expected goals. Knockouts that go to penalties are tracked as draws for accuracy purposes since extra time and penalties are inherently less predictable.

How has the model performed on soccer?

68% moneyline accuracy across 112 tracked games (60-28-24 W-D-L). See the full breakdown on the Results page.

Can I run my own World Cup matchup?

Yes — the soccer scoreboard lets you pick any two international teams instantly.
