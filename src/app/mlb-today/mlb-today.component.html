How to Read This Page

Projected Score
Expected final score for both teams based on starting pitchers, lineups, and ballpark factors.
Win Probability
The model's estimated chance the picked team wins outright. Higher % = stronger lean.
Confidence
Elite (70%+) · High (65–69%) · Medium (60–64%) · Low (<60%) — based on win probability.
Odds
Consensus moneyline for the picked team at time of generation. Compare to your book's line.

What the Model Uses

Each pick is powered by the same engine behind the scoreboard tool. The model projects expected runs from pitcher quality, lineup strength, and how the ballpark affects scoring — then ranks picks by the gap between model confidence and implied odds.
